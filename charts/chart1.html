?What is the Big Mac index?

The Big Mac index is a light-hearted way to ask whether a currency is too strong or too weak.

A Big Mac is almost the same everywhere, so its price should buy about the same in every country. The Economist converts each country’s Big Mac price into US dollars at the market exchange rate and compares it with the US price.

Example, July 2026, compared with the US dollar: Switzerland +45% means a Big Mac there costs 45% more than in the US, so the Swiss franc looks 45% overvalued. A minus means cheaper than the US (undervalued).

It is a simple rule of thumb, not an exact measure.

The “Compare with” buttons change who you compare with: pick the euro and every country is compared with the euro area instead of the US.
